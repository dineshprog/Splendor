Splendor — Rules & Mechanics

Goal

Be the first merchant to reach the target Prestige Points (15 Standard, 20 Extended, 25 Grandmaster, 30 Epic, or 35 Legendary). The round finishes so all players have had equal turns.

Turn Actions (Choose Exactly 1):

  • Take 3 Distinct Gem Tokens of different colors (excluding Gold).
  • Take 2 Gem Tokens of the same color (allowed ONLY if that stack has at least 4 tokens).
  • Reserve 1 Development Card (+1 Gold Joker) from the board or top of a tier deck. Max 3 reserved cards.
  • Purchase 1 Card from the board or your reserved hand. Permanent bonuses reduce the token cost. Gold wildcards substitute for any missing tokens.

Token Limit:

You may hold at most 10 tokens at the end of your turn. If you have more, you must discard the excess.

Nobles:

Nobles automatically visit you (+3 points) when your permanent card bonuses satisfy their requirements. If multiple qualify on the same turn, you choose one.

⚔️ Dynamic Game Modes:

  • 👑 Classic Splendor: Race to target prestige points (15-35 PTS). Standard Renaissance rules.
  • ⚡ Super Powers: 5 secret arcane powers drawn each match, sealed for the first 5 rounds. Starting in Round 6, the seals shatter and players unlock game-changing passives and game-breaking instant bursts through in-game feats!
  • 🖤 Shadow Syndicate: The first merchant to collect 10 Onyx (black) development cards wins immediately!
  • 🪜 Guild Ladder: True craftsmanship! You must own at least 6 Tier 1 cards to buy Tier 2 cards, and at least 6 Tier 2 cards to buy Tier 3 cards.
  • ⭐ Color Monopoly: First merchant to buy 5 cards in a color earns 1 Monopoly Trophy. The first player to earn 3 Trophies wins!
  • ⚜️ Noble Court: Nobles hold royal sway. The first merchant to attract 3 Noble Patrons wins the crown!
  • 🌈 Renaissance Prism: Prove universal mastery by acquiring at least 3 development cards in each of the 5 colors (15 cards total) to win!

Tiebreaker Hierarchy:

  1. Most Prestige Points
  2. Fewest purchased development cards
  3. Highest total tokens remaining
